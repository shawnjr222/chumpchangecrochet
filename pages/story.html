Our story

Howdy, neighbor!

Chump Change Crochet started at a folding table in a church basement — one hook, a bag of cotton yarn, and a firm belief that bare walls deserve better.

Every piece is still designed, hooked, blocked, and framed by hand in small batches. No restocks, no repeats, no two alike — the hand makes sure of that.

Thanks for stopping by the table. Stay for the lemonade.

— hooked with love, the Chump Change crew
