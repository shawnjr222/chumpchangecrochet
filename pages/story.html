Our Story

Chump Change Crochet started about four years ago as a new, self-taught hobby. What began as just a creative outlet quickly grew into something more with the encouragement and support of friends and family.

I've always loved the idea of making handmade clothing and home decor, and started out experimenting with crochet garments and custom commissions. Eventually, I discovered filet crochet and fell in love with its balance of simplicity and intricacy, and unmistakably vintage charm. Inspired by vintage linens, heirloom textiles, and old-fashioned home decor, I love the timelessness of traditional crochet and the charm of bringing a little nostalgia into modern spaces.

Every piece is crocheted, and carefully blocked by me in small batches. Thanks for stopping by and supporting handmade!

with love, Chump Change
