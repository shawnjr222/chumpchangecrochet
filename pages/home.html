    Hooked, blocked and framed by hand. Four short steps to ask for yours.

    1. Send a message to @chumpchangecrochet and start with “Custom piece” so it doesn’t get lost.

    2. The idea
      A photo or sketch helps most
      Size
      In inches, or the spot it is for
      Colors
      Ones you want, and any to avoid
      Frame
      Wood, white, black or blue
      Date
      When you need it
      Shipping to
      City and state, or country
      Budget
      A rough range

    3. You’ll hear from me with any questions, a price and a date it will be ready. Nothing after 3 days? Check your message requests, since Instagram sometimes hides first messages.

    4. Once you say yes, I hook, block and frame it by hand. It arrives ready to hang.
